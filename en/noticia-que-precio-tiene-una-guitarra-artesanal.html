What is the price of a handmade guitar?

Price of a handmade guitar Mariano Conde: from €4,235 to exclusive editions of €121,000. Quality, tradition and unique sound.

Workshop Mariano Conde

When they ask us about the price of ahandmade guitar, at Mariano Conde we answer that it depends deeply on the level of customization that the guitarist wants for his instrument. Here we are not talking about a simple object, but about a unique piece, which carries with it the technique, the soul of wood and the centuries-old tradition of our house.

Personalization and its impact on price

The value of a handmade guitar is not fixed because each musician looks for something different: wood, finish, measurements, aesthetics and personalized details. In our workshop, the options range from the most accessible classical concert and flamenco guitars, to exclusive pieces that become authentic works of art.

In theclassical concert guitars, the price starts at around 4,235 euros and can exceed 24,000 euros, depending on the noble woods chosen, the design of the neck, the finish and other technical elements. For example, the choice of a soundboard made of German spruce or red cedar, or a back and sides of rosewood, koa or exotic maple, greatly influences the cost and sonic personality of the instrument.

Ourflamenco guitars, built with Spanish cypress and selected pine or cedar top, have prices ranging from 4,235 euros to 12,100 euros or more in concert models. Like any handmade guitar, its price reflects the meticulous manual work, the rigorous selection of woods and the careful construction that guarantee comfort, lightness and an authentic and powerful sound for the flamenco touch.

Special editions: Poema Guitars and Double Cover

Beyond our standard ranges, we want to highlight two very special series that represent the maximum expression of our craft: the Poema Guitars Edition and the Double Cover Edition.

TheGuitar Edition of the Poemis a special series of unique pieces, each one a prototype of a new construction line that explores exceptional materials, such as woods that can have centuries of history. They are guitars valued at 48,400 euros (VAT included). Each one is an exclusive instrument, not only in its beauty and sound, but also in the experimentation and evolution that they represent within the tradition that we preserve with respect and passion.

Afterwards, theDouble Cover Editiontakes exclusivity even further with guitars of totally exclusive design, built with old woods, some of which can be centuries old. These unique pieces are valued at 121,000 euros (VAT included). They are the result of artisanal work in which we integrate innovation and respect for history, offering an instrument with unparalleled sound and aesthetic qualities.

The artisanal essence: woods and techniques

For us, the artisanal process begins with the selection of the wood. We use air-dried woods, some that have been drying naturally for between 15 and 20 years for our standard models, and even almost century-old woods in our most exclusive editions. This not only provides stability but also a millimetric tonal richness that industrial production cannot match.

The work of our hands, together with our experience, allows each guitar to have its own character, appropriate to the needs of the musician. We select each piece of wood, adjust the neck, define the string pull and polish until the guitar speaks with its own voice and responds to the subtlest touch.

Why the investment?

The price is also a reflection of the time invested, since making a handmade guitar can take months. Each stage, from the construction of the case to the final varnishing, is carried out with unique dedication. The investment is not only in materials, but in knowledge, decades of family tradition and precision.

In addition, each guitar bears individual marking, numbering and certification that ensures its authenticity and exclusivity. This fills us with pride and responsibility, because we know that we are giving a guitar for life, so that it transmits the entire essence of the person who plays it.

Conclusion: a fair price for a unique work

The price of a handmade guitar varies depending on multiple factors, but it always reflects the real value of an instrument that is much more than a product. It is a living legacy, a perfect balance between tradition, art and technique, made especially for you.

At Mariano Conde, we invite you to discover for yourself the experience of choosing your handmade guitar, the one that best suits your personality and music. From our classic guitars to the most exclusive editions such as the Poema Guitars or the Double Top, we assure you an instrument that will be your faithful companion and a sonic jewel for life.

Mariano Conde
