Canadian red cedar wood for classical and flamenco guitar

Discover the unique properties of Canadian red cedar in guitars: warm sound, fast response and constant evolution. Ideal for superior quality soundboards.

Workshop Mariano Conde

Canadian red cedar (Thuja plicata), also known as "Western Red Cedar" or Pacific red cedar, is one of the most valued woods for its beauty, durability and exceptional properties, being widely used in various applications from construction to the manufacture of musical instruments.

Physical characteristics

Density and color

Canadian red cedar has grayish-white sapwood, while its freshly cut heartwood is saffron yellow or dark chocolate brown, which turns a more uniform reddish brown when exposed to air. When exposed to the elements, it acquires a characteristic silver-gray color, highly appreciated for its beauty. It is a remarkably light wood, with a density between 330-390 kg/m³.

Structure

It has a distinctive and pleasant aroma, similar to that of cedar, with a slightly bitter flavor. Its structure presents little nervous contraction, which contributes to its excellent dimensional stability. Red cedar has a fiber saturation point moisture content of 18 to 23%, compared to 25 to 30% for most Canadian softwoods.

Sound properties

Warm and deep sound

In musical instruments such as the classical guitar, the cedar top provides a rounder and velvety sound, with very powerful bass and rich in harmonics. It offers a fast response, meaning that the sound develops quickly when you pluck the string.

Sound evolution

A distinctive characteristic of cedar is its great sound capacity from its birth. A good cedar guitar top should have a quality sound from the first moment, there is an urban legend that says that cedar stagnates over time or that it has no sound evolution

FALSE!

From our point of view, a good guitar, well built with the techniques and knowledge it requires and with good and dry material, will always sound good from its birth and will constantly evolve.

Technical aspects

Structural properties

  • Excellent dimensional stability due to its low density and low shrinkage factor
  • High natural resistance to moisture and rot
  • Minimal shrinkage and swelling with humidity changes
  • Classified as durable against the action of fungi

Workability

Red cedar is a soft wood and very easy to work with.

Sawing is easy and dulling of the saws is normal, but it is recommended to work with very sharp tools due to the fragility of the ends. It requires care when working and once the guitar is finished since it is a wood that cracks easily.

Practical considerations

Availability

It is found on the west coast of North America, from Alaska to California. Its production and export are stable. The North American Indians called it "the tree of life", since they used it to make dresses, baskets, houses and canoes.

Ideal applications

Canadian red cedar is particularly suitable for:

  • Soundboards for classical and flamenco guitars, especially for musicians who prefer a warmer tone.
  • Interior and exterior carpentry
  • Manufacture of pencils, being the most used wood for this purpose

This exceptional wood combines natural beauty, durability and unique acoustic properties, making it a premium choice for numerous applications where both aesthetics and performance are valued.
