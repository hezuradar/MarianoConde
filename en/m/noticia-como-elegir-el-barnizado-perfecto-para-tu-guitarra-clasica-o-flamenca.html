How to choose the perfect varnish for your classical or flamenco guitar

Discover the types of varnish for Mariano Conde guitars: finishes that enhance aesthetics, protect the wood and improve resonance.

Workshop Mariano Conde

Varnishing is one of the most important decisions in the construction of aguitar, since it not only defines its aesthetics, but also directly influences the resonance, feel and protection of the wood. At Mariano Conde, we offer a selection of finishes that allow each instrument to be customized according to the taste and acoustic needs of the musician. Each type of varnish has unique characteristics that can make a difference both in the visual aspect and in the experience of playing and listening to the guitar.

Available types of varnishing

Red, orange and gold

These finishes provide a vibrant and distinctive visual touch to the guitar, providing personality and stage presence. They are ideal for musicians looking for an instrument with character. Additionally, these colors intensify the perception of the shine and depth of the finish, making the guitar stand out in any setting.

Varnishing in red, orange or gold is generally done with synthetic varnishes, which guarantee a resistant and long-lasting protective layer against wear and humidity.

Colorless

This varnish highlights the natural color of the wood, showing its beauty and grain without alterations. It is perfect for those who prefer a classic and elegant look.

Clear varnish is usually used on concert guitars and on instruments where the wood is of high quality, as it allows you to appreciate every detail of the grain and grain. In addition, it protects the wood without modifying its color, preserving the original essence of the instrument.

Matte colorless

Similar to the colorless one, but with a matte finish that softens the shine and provides a more discreet and modern appearance. It is ideal for those looking for a sober and acoustic look.

This finish is highly appreciated by guitarists who want a contemporary and minimalist aesthetic. In addition, it reduces light reflections, facilitating concentration during performance.

Shellac doll

It is the artisanal finish par excellence, applied by hand in multiple thin layers. It highlights the acoustic transparency of the wood and enhances the nuances of the sound, especially in medium and high frequencies.

It is preferred in high-end guitars, although it requires more work time and has an additional cost of €500. Each layer is applied manually, allowing complete control over the thickness and uniformity of the varnish, enhancing the natural vibration of the wood.

Features and benefits of each option

The red, orange or gold finish is perfect for those looking for a guitar with personality and a strong visual presence. It offers a very resistant protective layer and an intense shine that highlights the design of the instrument.

The colorless finish is the most classic and elegant option. It allows you to appreciate the natural beauty of the wood and is highly valued in concert guitars, as it protects without altering the original color of the instrument.

The colorless matte finish provides a more modern and discreet aesthetic, ideal for those looking for a sober and functional instrument, also reducing light reflections during performance.

Shellac doll is the most artisanal and acoustically superior finish. It enhances sound nuances and offers a more open, natural and nuanced sound, making it the preferred choice for high-end guitars.

Choice of varnishing

The choice of varnish depends on the balance between aesthetics, protection and sound. Synthetic finishes (red, orange, gold, colorless and matte) offer high protection and quick application, while dolly shellac is the more traditional and acoustically superior option, although more delicate and expensive.

Each guitar can be customized to reflect the identity of the musician, ensuring a unique instrument in both appearance and sound.

If you're looking for a finish that combines beauty, sound and craftsmanship, shellac is the ultimate choice for concert guitars and discerning hobbyists. For a modern and practical look, red, orange, gold, colorless and matte finishes offer versatility and durability without sacrificing style.
