  1. 1915

    Domingo Esteso opens the workshop

    In Madrid, his grandfather founded the dynasty of guitar makers that would give rise to Conde Hermanos.

    After years of observing and asking his father Mariano and his uncle Faustino, he built his first complete instrument.

  3. 1980

  4. 1983

    Mariano takes the reins

    He takes over the family business with Faustino, maintaining the traditional construction method.

  5. 1986

    The company is formed that will take the guitars from the house to the great stages of the world.

Story of Mariano Conde

«Between guitars
"My teeth came out."

School manual work, observation and questions directed to my father Mariano and my uncle Faustino about how, why and why of the different tools. Observation and questions. Many questions. Curiosity to know how a guitar is built from the moment the raw material, the tree, falls to the ground in a more than exciting guitar "Latin".

Little by little I got to know wood, the use of files, chisels, brushes and clubs, cutting and drying wood, templates and markers, rulers and measurements, different forms of assembly and an exhaustive familiarization with glues, tools and materials. All of this resulted in the first guitar built entirely by me seeing the light back in 1976.

On December 23, 1980, I picked up the opening license for the new family workshop located on Felipe V Street at the Madrid Town Hall, and opened its doors. My uncle Faustino was listed as the applicant. In those early years the guitars I built with my father were labeled Gravina number 7, since Gravina was still the parent company; In 1984, a few already carried the Felipe V, 2 label.

In 1983, upon my father's retirement, it was his and my uncle Faustino's decision that I, even though I was the youngest, take charge of the business: thus breaking with the ownership. In 1986 I made the decision to create the community of property Conde Hermanos, which materialized on December 30, in which I incorporated my brother voluntarily. From 1986 to 1989 I built twelve guitars with my autograph signature, the initials M and F of Mariano Félix, as an act of vindication of authorship.

Conde Hermanos, C.B. It remained operational until the dissolution agreement in 2009 and definitively on April 30, 2010. After the dissolution I settled with my son Mariano and the employees of the defunct company in the premises on Amnistía street, 1 of Madrid. Since the first, the labels of all Mariano Conde guitars are made and written by hand: they indicate name, street, model, serial number and year of completion.

Here the Poema Guitars were developed and the instruments began to be marked with the initials “MC”, first with a marker and then with fire, so that there was no room for error about the authorship: the half-moon head was used by my family and it was necessary to identify its origin. These ideas and their reasoning are registered under the name “Initials” in the Intellectual Property Registry of Madrid.

Since 2018, Mariano Conde father and son have been working on their guitars without the help of any official or operator, their own or external. Our roots are anchored in the oldest guitar school in Madrid: we are authentic half-moon guitars and ancient Esteso–Conde tradition.

The real half moon

A shape that hasn't moved in a century

The half-moon head, the fire-etched initials MC and the hand-numbered label are the signature of a house that has preferred to grow slowly rather than lose its identity.

Mariano Conde's guitar has accompanied Paco de Lucía, Paco Cepero, Rafael Riqueni, Raimundo Amador, Leonard Cohen, Bob Dylan, Lenny Kravitz and Ed Sheeran, among others.
