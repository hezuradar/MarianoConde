Why some guitars “open up” over time

Over time, many guitars experience a noticeable change in their sound, becoming more resonant, open, and nuanced. This phenomenon, known as“open up”, is highly appreciated by guitarists and luthiers, as it marks the acoustic evolution of the instrument.

Below, we explain why some guitars “open up” over time and how this process benefits sound quality.

What does it mean for a guitar to “open”?

When it is said that a guitar “opens”, it means that its sound improves with use and the passage of years. The guitar gains projection, clarity and tonal richness, especially in the mid and high frequencies.

This change is not immediate, but occurs gradually thanks to the constant vibration of the wood and the natural aging of the instrument.

Factors that influence the “open” of a guitar

Constant vibration

Frequent use of the guitar causes the wood to vibrate and “tune” itself acoustically. Over time, the top and internal structures respond better to the attack of the strings, enhancing resonance and projection.

Wood aging

Wood, when exposed to air and humidity, adapts and stabilizes. This process allows the pores and fibers to relax, facilitating better transmission of vibrations and improving tone.

Varnishing stabilization

Varnishing, especially on handmade guitars, also evolves over time. The layers settle and allow the wood to vibrate more freely, contributing to a more open and nuanced sound.

Construction quality

Well-built guitars, with selected woods and balanced bracing, are more likely to “open” correctly. A good structural design favors the acoustic evolution of the instrument.

Benefits of “open” on the guitar

  • Greater projection:The sound becomes more powerful and clear, ideal for concerts and recordings.
  • Better tonal balance:Mid and high frequencies are boosted, while low frequencies become more defined.
  • Richness of nuances:The instrument gains expressiveness, allowing a greater dynamic range.
  • Unique experience:Each guitar “opens” differently, becoming a unique instrument.

How to favor the “open” of a guitar

  • Frequent use:Playing the guitar regularly helps the wood adapt and respond better.
  • Care and maintenance:Keeping the guitar in a stable environment, with adequate humidity and temperature, favors its acoustic evolution.
  • Patience:The process of “opening up” can take months or years, so it is important to enjoy the gradual change.

Conclusion

That a guitar “opens” over time is a natural and desirable phenomenon, a result of the interaction between wood, construction and use. Well-made and well-cared for guitars are more likely to develop a more open, nuanced and expressive sound, becoming unique and special instruments.
