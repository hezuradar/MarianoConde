How do we work them?

What weinspire.

Since my childhood I have had literary interests. Prose, and especially verse, have accompanied me in all stages of my life. From that childhood at the Escuelas Pías de San Antón school, where I led a team of classmates who I urged to write a short story daily, until in 2009 “Anhelo” emerged, the first of the Guitars of the Poem.

The first was beheaded and dissolved after being called to the rector's office due to complaints from the parents of my classmates, who claimed not to do their homework because they had to write the stories that they presented to me the next morning. The second is already in verse number 58 displayed on the label of a Guitar of the Poem Mariano Conde, after a slow and constant numerical trickle upwards.

I place the Guitars of the Poem at the upper vertex of an imaginary rhombus, as explained in the verse of “Rombo”, guitar number 56 in the series. Vertex and crucible of good guitars where there is no distinction between flamenco and classical concert guitars, where pop, jazz and Brazilian rhythms, among other musical currents, have a place.

The key to all of the above lies in the construction of these guitars: exhaustive craftsmanship in which each guitar has its own constructive personality, taking into account the flexibility or rigidity of the woods that make it up, some of which are centuries old. Tests, verifications and extremely fine craftsmanship that delay its construction for months.

Wood speaks, and listening to it and understanding it is the goal. There are no better woods or worse woods. The wood is there, waiting, and the more years it waits, the better. “De Todo”, number 33, is made with eleven different woods and begins the metamorphosis of the half moon into a “full moon”.

As for the theme of the verses, I can say that it is very varied: there are flamenco sentences, brief biographies, descriptions, romantic and eschatological nuances. The Poema Guitars are in the hands of professional and amateur guitarists, usually with one or two units, and collectors with up to four. Its owners are located in España, Australia, Germany, Türkiye, Holland, France, Nicaragua or Thailand, among other countries. Among the professionals, Amós Lora, Jesús Guerrero, Ed Sheeran, Raimundo Amador, Joni Jiménez and Antonio Sánchez stand out.
