The emotion of a guitar with soul: Samuel del Río Carmona, first winner of the “Mariano Conde” award at the International Cante de las Minas Festival

Samuel del Río Carmona, first winner of the “Mariano Conde” guitar prize at Cante de las Minas 2025: tradition, emotion and future of flamenco.

Workshop Mariano Conde

Talk aboutInternational Festival of Cante de las Minasis to talk about the living history of flamenco. For more than six decades, this event held in La Unión, Murcia, has gone from being a local meeting to becoming an authentic cathedral of flamenco art. Here the greats come and here the young people who carry within them the heritage of centuries are consecrated. It is no coincidence that artists who are legends today have found the definitive boost for their careers on these boards.

The 2025 edition will not only be remembered for the brilliance of the singers, dancers and musicians, not even for the expected victory ofGregorio Moya, the singer from Argamasilla de Alba who won the prestigiousMining Lamp, the festival's highest award. This year will also be remembered for the exciting establishment of a new recognition that bears our name: the“Mariano Conde” guitar award, delivered for the first time in this edition. And what for us is a source of pride comes accompanied by the satisfaction of having placed our hands and our guitar tradition in those of the future: the young man from CadizSamuel del Río Carmonawas honored with this historic award, thus becoming hisfirst winner.

The importance of a prize with soul

When we think about flamenco, quejíos and ancestral palos inevitably come to mind. But there is an invisible thread that ties it all together: the guitar. Without it, cante would be left naked, without that spine that supports it, without that dialogue that goes beyond the musical and delves into the spiritual.

Therefore, when the Festival decided to take a step forward and create the “Mariano Conde” guitar award, we saw in it something more than a simple distinction. It is not only about recognizing the best accompanying guitarist or soloist of the competition, but about highlighting the tradition, roots and craftsmanship that lies behind each note.

The organization of the event itself enthusiastically sharedthe ad on your Instagram profile, underlining the historical significance of this new award, which was born with the intention of lasting. And there was no shortage of videos where the emotion of the moment was reflected, such asthe reel with the ovation when receiving the award.

Samuel del Río Carmona: youth and talent from Cádiz

The jury was clear: among the participants in this edition,Samuel del Río CarmonaHe shone with exquisite accompaniment, showing technical mastery and sensitivity rare in a guitarist of his age. Born in Cádiz, a land where every corner breathes rhythm, Samuel represents a new batch of musicians who, without forgetting what they learned from the old masters, seek their own language to dialogue with singers and dancers.

On the La Unión stage, his touch was pure and at the same time personal. He knew how to accompany without invading and give prominence to each suit with just the right force. The public, understood like few others in flamenco sensibility, recognized him with heartfelt ovations. Critics also noted it among the highlights of the edition. And finally, the festival gave him the firstGuitar Mariano Conde, an instrument that is not only an award but also a traveling companion.

The moment of delivery

Those of us who were there could feel it in the air: there was expectation, there was history becoming present. The moment of handing over the guitar was brief in time, but immense in meaning. Samuel received her with a mixture of pride and contained emotion. In his eyes, and in the still young wood of the instrument, you could read everything that was to come.

That moment was recorded thanks to various specialized media - such asthis emotional video shared by a flamenco page, where you can see the delivery of our guitar at Samuel del Río—, and reminded us of the essential thing: it is not just about building guitars; It is building bridges between tradition and the future, between the craftsman who shapes the sound and the artist who elevates it towards the soul of the public.

The echo of the festival in 2025

Beyond our award, this 2025 edition of the International Cante de las Minas Festival had memorable moments. The triumph of Gregorio Moya, the true protagonist of the edition, crowned a few days where the purest singing was mixed with innovation without losing authenticity.

The national press echoed it. Media likeThe VanguardThey highlighted that the singer“swept away” at the Festival, whileEuropa Presstold howwon the long-awaited Mining Lamp.

There was also echo in the Andalusian press, with headlines that highlightedartist awards such as José Viñas, Samuel del Río and Encarni, reflecting the breadth of talents that come together in La Unión every year.

A guitar as a symbol

For those who are unaware of the intimate universe of guitar construction, it may seem like something merely material: an instrument is handed over and that's it. But each guitar that leaves our workshop contains hundreds of hours of work, precision, and listening. As one of our elders said,“each guitar is born with a part of us inside”.

Therefore, delivering a guitar in La Unión is not the same as anywhere else. It is giving the young guitarist a witness loaded with memory. It is saying: here is your tool to continue walking, but also here is a fragment of us, of our history and of everything that before you has made it possible.

Samuel understood it perfectly. We saw it in the way he caressed his new partner, in how he embraced her with that reverential respect that good musicians always have for their instruments.

Looking to the future

The Mariano Conde award has just been born, but we already feel that it has a long journey ahead of it. Year after year, a new guitar will be named after a young talent. Year after year, the tradition will continue to pass from hands to hands, keeping the heartbeat of flamenco alive.

We dream that in twenty or thirty years, when the history of this award is remembered, it will be said that the first to receive it was Samuel del Río Carmona, a young man from Cádiz who knew how to move us with his playing and who since then embarked on a path in which his guitar and flamenco were inseparable.

And we, as builders who have had the privilege of being present at that first moment, can only thank the Festival del Cante de las Minas for the opportunity to have added our voice to this chorus of art and emotion.

Conclusion

The 2025 edition of the International Cante de las Minas Festival will be remembered as a milestone in its already long history. The triumph of Gregorio Moya will be recorded in the annals of the contest, but so will the birth of an award that combines craftsmanship, music and feeling: theGuitar Mariano Conde.

That instrument, given toSamuel del Río Carmona, it is not just a trophy. It is a bridge between generations, a symbol of the union between La Unión and Cádiz, between the workshop and the stage, between wood and soul. In short, it is a metaphor for flamenco itself: an art that never stops renewing itself, always faithful to its roots but constantly open to the future.

From our workshop, we will continue to put our hands and our hearts so that each guitar that is born has within it a little of that duende that unites all those who love this art. And we trust that Samuel, with his award in his hands, will continue to open paths so that the flamenco guitar continues to be what it always was: a heartbeat that never dies.

Mariano Conde
