Handmade guitars: tradition, art and excellence in Mariano Conde

Unique artisanal guitars, handmade in Mariano Conde: tradition, noble materials and exceptional sound for demanding musicians.

Workshop Mariano Conde

Handcrafted guitars represent the maximum expression of lutheria, where each instrument is the result of the dedication, passion and experience of generations of artisans. At Mariano Conde, we celebrate this tradition by offering unique guitars, handcrafted with selected materials and techniques that guarantee unmatched sound and timeless beauty.

What makes a handmade guitar special?

  • Each guitar is uniquea: No two handmade guitars are the same. Eachtype of wood, every detail in the finish and every adjustment is carefully made by us, ensuring an instrument with its own personality.
  • Top quality materials: We use noble woods such as cedar, Indian rosewood, ebony or fir, selected for their sound, stability and natural beauty.
  • Artisanal process: Building a handcrafted guitar involves total control at every stage, from design to final polishing. We integrate the best classical technology available, but always prioritizing quality and the human touch over industrial processes.
  • Exceptional sound: The result is an instrument with a projection, richness of nuances and comfort that mass production cannot match. That's why professional and amateur musicians choose handcrafted guitars to express their art.
  • dry woods: in a traditional way over the years, without drying rooms or new inventions. Our simplest guitar is built with wood that has been dried for 15-20 years and this increases as we go up in range, model and price until we reach the point of using almost century-old woods like those used in our highest model “Guitars of the Poem”.

Advantages of choosing a handmade guitar in Mariano Conde

  • Personalized advice: We help you choose the guitar that best suits your style and needs, whether you are a beginner or a concert performer.
  • Personalization: We offer the possibility of customizing your guitar, choosing woods, finishes and unique details.
  • Quality guarantee: Each guitar goes through rigorous quality control to ensure durability, comfort and sonic excellence.
  • Tradition and prestige: Our brand is synonymous with tradition, innovation and international recognition in the world of handmade guitar.

Tips for choosing your handmade guitar

  • Define your musical style: ¿concert classic, flamencoor ofPoem? Each type of guitar has specific characteristics in terms of sound and construction.
  • Try different models: Comfort and sound are key factors. We invite you to visit our workshop or consult with our experts to find the perfect guitar.
  • Value the history and the process: Knowing the history behind each instrument and the luthier's work adds emotional and artistic value to your choice.

The experience Mariano Conde

In Mariano Conde, you not only acquire a guitar, but you immerse yourself in a unique experience, where tradition, innovation and passion for music come together to create exceptional instruments. Discover our collection of handmade guitars and let yourself be captivated by the art of luthiery.

The value of handmade guitars: economic and sentimental

In Mariano Conde, handcrafted guitars are more than instruments: they are works of art that convey history, emotion and sonic excellence.

sentimental value

The sentimental value of a handmade guitar is, for many musicians, incalculable. Each instrument is unique, not only because of its construction and materials, but because it is usually custom made, reflecting the personality and needs of the guitarist. This bond is strengthened for several reasons:

  • The handmade guitar accompanies the musician in his artistic development, witnessing his achievements, emotions and experiences.
  • The creation process is usually collaborative, allowing the musician to participate in the choice of woods, finishes and details, which generates a deep emotional connection with the instrument.
  • The handmade guitar is capable of transmitting emotions and feelings in a unique way, becoming an extension of the artist himself and a channel to share his passion with the public.

As a result, these guitars are not only musical tools, but true life companions, loaded with memories and meaning. Their sentimental value grows over time, and they often become family heirloom objects, transmitting not only music, but also history and tradition.

Economic value

Our handcrafted guitars, classical concert, flamenco and poem guitars, are instruments whose price reflects much more than the simple cost of the materials. The economic value of a handmade guitar is usually, in the case of a professional luthier, above 4,000 euros, and can considerably increase the exclusivity of the materials and the level of customization. This price includes:

  • High quality materials, individually selected for each instrument.
  • The experience of the luthier, who dedicates dozens or hundreds of hours to each guitar, ensuring an exceptional finish and sound for more than 110 years, transmitting and evolving from generation to generation.
