Maintenance of a guitar Mariano Conde

Discover how to keep your guitar in optimal condition. Expert advice on cleaning, storage and adjustments to preserve its sound and structure.

Workshop Mariano Conde

Proper care of aguitarrequires meticulous attention to several fundamental aspects.

Basic daily care

Cleaning after each use is essential.Use a soft, dry cloth to remove fingerprints, sweat and dust from the surface. This will prevent dirt buildup and protect the finish of the instrument.

Control of conditions

The right environment is crucial to preserving the instrument. Hold the guitar:

  • Away from sudden changes in temperature
  • In a controlled humidity environment
  • Protected from direct sunlight

Fretboard Maintenance

The fingerboard is the only wood that is not protected with varnish, therefore it is a wood that has open pores and since Mariano Conde we do not recommend in any case the use of any cleaning liquid, or magic oils that are so fashionable.

Periodic adjustments

Regular Review

Check regularly:

  • Scratches and bumps that can lead to fissures and cracks.
  • Good greasing between “the crown and the worm” of the tuners.

Proper storage

When not in use, the guitar must:

  • Store in its rigid case or custom-made display case.
  • Stay upright
  • Locate in a place with stable temperature
  • Do not put the guitar in the upper part of a closet since the heat is concentrated in the upper and higher parts of the rooms.
  • If you do not use the guitar for a long period of time, two things are recommended: lower the tension of the strings one or two tones (it is not advisable to remove them) and open the case periodically so that it has good ventilation.

Preventive maintenance

Regular inspection helps identify potential problems before they become serious. Periodically check for signs of wear on:

  • Frets
  • bridge
  • tuning system
  • Electrical connections (in case of electroacoustic guitars)

With this systematic care, the instrument will maintain its sound and structural qualities for many years.

Professional service

To guarantee the optimal condition of your instrument, we invite you to visit our specialized guitar shop. Mariano Conde father & son are trained to perform everything from basic adjustments to complex repairs, ensuring that your guitar maintains its sonic excellence and construction quality. We have the tools and knowledge necessary to treat high-end instruments with the care they deserve. Don't hesitate to contact us to schedule a professional inspection or maintenance.

Mariano Conde guitars have a lifetime guarantee as long as they are well preserved and are inspected, repaired or restored by us if necessary in our own home by Mariano, son or father. This guarantee would be lost if it is manipulated by other guitar makers.

Likewise and for this same reason we do not repair guitars from builders who are active under any circumstances.
