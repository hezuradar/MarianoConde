A workshop with open doors

Since 2018, Mariano and his son have worked together without workers outside the family. You can follow the process of building a guitar live or visit us at the Madrid center.

The YouTube channel (@Guitarras_MarianoConde) and Twitch (guitarrasmarianoconde) maintain the live workshop as a permanent window to the craft.
